Data Deletion Request

Manage and request deletion of data associated with your use of our apps. We are committed to your privacy and transparency.

Important Notice: Sumil Soft does not directly collect or store your personal data. However, our apps may use third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, etc.) that may collect data such as activity history, transaction history, device identifiers, and images or video for ad-serving purposes. This page helps you request deletion of that data.

📋 What Data May Be Collected

The following types of data may be collected by third-party advertisers integrated into our apps. We do not have direct access to or control over this data.

Activity History Transaction History Device Identifiers Images & Video Ad Interaction Data Approximate Location Usage Analytics

🗑️ How to Request Data Deletion

Follow these steps to request deletion of data collected by advertisers through our apps:

  1. Opt Out of Personalized Ads On Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID. On iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable Allow Apps to Request to Track.
  2. Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID. This prevents advertisers from linking future data to your profile.
  3. Request Deletion via Google (AdMob) Visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy and submit a data deletion request. If you use other ad networks, visit their respective privacy portals.
  4. Contact Us Directly Email us at support@sumilsoft.com with your device ID or registered email. We will forward your request to the relevant third-party ad providers on your behalf.
  5. Uninstall the App Uninstalling the app stops any further data collection through it. Previously collected data by advertisers must be deleted via their own portals (steps above).

🔒 Our Commitment

Sumil Soft does not sell, trade, or share your personal information. Any data collected through third-party advertising SDKs is governed by those advertisers' own privacy policies.

For further inquiries about data privacy or to escalate a deletion request, please reach out to us directly. We aim to respond within 7 business days.
